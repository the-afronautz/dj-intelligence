## Adding yourself

1. Read the [CLA](./CLA.md).
2. Either email the maintainer the signature block from that document, or open
   a pull request adding a row to the table above.
3. Use your full legal name — the same one you would sign a contract with, not
   a handle or nickname.

Your first code contribution can be merged once your row appears here.
