## Why this exists

DJ Intelligence is open source and intended to stay that way. At the same time,
parts of this work may become a commercial product in the future.

Without this agreement, every contributor would hold veto power over any change
to the project's license, since each contributor owns the copyright in their own
contributions. That means a project can become permanently locked to its
original license once a contributor becomes unreachable.

This agreement keeps those options open while leaving you full ownership of
what you wrote. It is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Individual
Contributor License Agreement, which is the most widely used template of its
kind.
